    01
    
    Introduction & Key Terms
    
    
    The vocabulary that separates a professional from a trespasser.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    What it is
    
      
      
        
    • Ethical hacking is the process of legally breaking into systems, networks or applications to find vulnerabilities that malicious hackers can exploit.
      • 
        
    • Goal — identify weaknesses and help organizations fix them before attackers do.
      • 
        
    • Also called — penetration testing.
      • 
      
    
      
    
        MOTTO
        “Think like a hacker, act like a professional.”

    03
    
    Information Gathering
    
    
    Two ways to learn about a target: never touch it, or touch it carefully.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    01 · PASSIVE
    
      
    Without interacting
    
      
    No packets sent to the target. Invisible in their logs.
    
      
      
        
    • Whois lookup
    • Google dorking
    • Social media
    • Website / DNS / IP info
      • 
      
    
      
    whois acme-corp.com
    
      
    site:acme-corp.com filetype:pdf
    
    
    

    
    
      
    02 · ACTIVE
    
      
    Interacting directly
    
      
    You send traffic. It is loud, it is logged — stay inside scope.
    
      
      
        
    • Ping sweep
    • Port scanning
    • Network mapping
    • Banner grabbing
      • 
      
    
      
    nmap -sn 10.10.0.0/24
    
      
    nc -v acme-corp.com 22
    
    
    
  
    

  
    OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) belongs to the passive column — everything the internet has already published about your target.

        CIA Triad
        
      
        
        
        
        
      
        
    
        

    
        
      
        Important tips
        
      
          
        
        1. Always get permission — in writing, before anything else.
          2. 
        
        2. Follow the rules of engagement, to the letter.
          3. 
        
        3. Document everything as you go, not afterwards.
          4. 
        
        4. Think like an attacker, act like a defender.
          5. 
        
        5. Keep learning and stay updated.
          6. 
      
        
      
        
        No authorization = no engagement. Unauthorized access is a crime regardless of intent.
